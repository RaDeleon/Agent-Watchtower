# Claude Watchtower 0.9.0

Stage 10: character attributes, and levels past 99. Everything is still computed by the rollup and stored in the local database; the pages only read and label it.

**Attributes.** The Character page gains six D&D attributes. Strength is output tokens per active hour, across Claude, Codex and Hermes. Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma each average two or three of the ten Usage Profile disciplines. Each has a Form from 0 to 99, read over the last 30 days and over the last 7. Each has a Base level, trained by every day's hours times that day's 30-day Form, so good habits level faster than hours alone. Each also has a Now level: Base plus a boost or drain of up to ten levels, from this week's Form against the month's. The card names your weakest attribute and the quests that train it.

**Past 99.** Levels continue to 200, at 250 more hours per level after Lv 99's 2,500. Four new tiers follow Master: Grandmaster at 120, Legend at 150, Mythic at 170 and Eternal at 200. A global level cap (99, then 120, 150, 170 and 200) rises on the day the first stat of any kind reaches it: a tool, a sub-skill or an attribute. The cap is a milestone and a display ceiling, never a limit on XP. Every cap raise appears in the level-up log. Pace shows the days to the current cap. Max Cape on Overview counts the tools at 99 or more.

**Schema.** The database moves to schema v11, adding `claude_discipline_scores_7d`, `character_attributes`, `level_caps` and `skill_pace.eta_cap_days`. A v10 database migrates in place on the next hook call or rollup, leaving its rows as they were, and the next rollup fills the new tables. Until then the Attributes card reads "pending".

Requires macOS or Linux with `bash`, `jq` and the `sqlite3` CLI, and Node.js 22.18 or newer with default TypeScript stripping. This is a preview: measurements and page layouts may still change between minor versions.
